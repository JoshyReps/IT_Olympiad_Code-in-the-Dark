Code in the Dark has two phases:
Coding Phase—Participants are only allowed to code during this phase and they cannot see
their outputs.
Review Phase—Participants are allowed to see their outputs and code during this phase, but
they cannot make changes.
The event comprises three rounds. The level of difculty of the design to
replicate will also increase for each round.

Easy Round
● The coding phase will last for 15 minutes.
● After that, the participants will proceed to the review phase, which will last for 1 minute.
● It will be followed by another coding phase that will last for 5 minutes.
● After the second coding phase, the judges will score the outputs.

Medium Round
● The coding phase will last for 20 minutes.
●After that, the participants will proceed to the next phase, which will last for 2 minutes. This
phase will be a combination of the coding and review phases, where the participants are allowed
to code while seeing their outputs.
● After this phase, the judges will score the outputs.

Hard Round
● The coding phase will last for 25 minutes. Participants will code from memory during this phase.
From the 5-minute to the
10-minute mark and from the 15-minute to the 20-minute mark, the Figma design will be hidden
from the participants. They are also not allowed to take a photo or draw the Figma design in any
medium as a guide.
● After the coding phase, they will proceed to the review phase, which will last for 2 minutes.
● It will be followed by another coding phase that will last for 5 minutes.
● After the second coding phase, the judges will score the outputs.
